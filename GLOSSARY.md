# Record collection

A photographic record room preserves the family's sleeves in their original box order.

## Language

**Box**: One of the five photographed groups of sleeves in the record room.

**Sleeve**: A physical catalogued item in a box, including the art book kept among the records. A folded edge may belong to the same sleeve as a neighboring source position.

**Box browsing**: Exploring sleeves in their physical box order, previewing a sleeve, pulling it out to inspect it, and putting it back before returning to the room.
