## Artwork policy

Clean documented external LP or book faces are preferred. The intake selects 56 external/reused faces for 28 items, including the mono Between the Buttons jacket and Canadian layouts where available. Existing good artwork is retained on the other anchors. Two rare classical outer backs (Trios WGS8196 and Messiah WGS8163-3) use actual-copy photo fallbacks after external searches failed. Crop calibration and full-resolution HEIC processing are documented; natural paper wear and lighting remain, with capture metadata omitted. The Views book rear uses a documented external cover cropped from its dealer photograph. No printed content was generated or reconstructed.

Per-face archive URL, source SHA, resulting asset SHA, dimensions, edition differences and visual review are in `artwork-assets.json` and the three artwork proposal manifests. External artwork is never labelled as a photograph of the family copy.

## Listening decisions

22 additional verified album IDs bring the total to 283, preserving all 261 previous verified links. Complete Spotify track lists were fetched independently and compared with physical programmes and performers. Expanded editions and mastering differences are disclosed. Seven newly identified/confirmed albums retain search because a complete matching album or exact take was not established:

- **Ten Wheel Drive with Genya Ravan — Brief Replies** (`upper-left-50`): No public Spotify page for the complete original album was verified. Exact album-title searches, the Ten Wheel Drive artist discography, and original-song parent-album leads yield The Best of Ten Wheel Drive, an incomplete compilation; it is rejected as an album substitute.
- **Ten Wheel Drive with Genya Ravan — Construction #1** (`upper-left-54`): No public Spotify page for the complete original album was verified. Exact album-title searches, the Ten Wheel Drive artist discography, and original-song parent-album leads yield The Best of Ten Wheel Drive, an incomplete compilation; it is rejected as an album substitute.
- **Lightdreams — Islands in Space** (`upper-middle-23`): No complete original seven-track Islands in Space album was verified. The exact artist/title Spotify result is a one-track 2013 single, not the 1981 LP; 10,001Dreams is a different Lightdreams album.
- **Leo Kottke — Leo Kottke** (`upper-middle-39`): No complete self-titled 1976 Chrysalis CHR1106 album was verified. Parent checked the artist’s full native Canadian Spotify discography (28 releases); the available Chrysalis anthologies are partial programmes and include live versions.
- **Walt Disney Productions / Various — The Mouse Factory Presents Mickey and His Friends** (`lower-middle-44`): No full twelve-part The Mouse Factory Presents Mickey and His Friends Disneyland1342 programme was verified. Minnie’s Yoo Hoo appears in Disney Classics, but that anthology omits the rest of this LP programme.
- **Hellmut Schneidewind / Wolfgang Pasch / Heinz Zickler / Herbert Thal / Edward H. Tarr; Mainz Chamber Orchestra / Günter Kehr; Württemberg Chamber Orchestra / Jörg Faerber — Italian Baroque Trumpet Concerti** (`lower-middle-53`): The photographed TV34057S mixed-player five-concerto programme was not verified on one digital album. Correct original Zickler/Thal/Kehr performances occur on Virtuoso Trumpet Vol.2, while Tarr/Faerber and Schneidewind/Pasch material is split across other volumes. Same-title recordings by other soloists were excluded.
- **Django Reinhardt & Stéphane Grappelli — The Quintet of the Hot Club of France** (`right-04`): Two Hallmark digital albums reproduce all fourteen GNP-9001 titles in exactly the original sequence, but neither identifies the Nuages master/take. The physical rear explicitly distinguishes a repeat take with the verse omitted and a different Django solo. A title/sequence/duration match alone does not prove that performance. Keep search-only under the exact-performance rule unless an independent publisher or audio comparison resolves the take.

## Reproduction and validation

The committed canonical catalog and public artwork are sufficient to serve the website. For replay of the intake, preserve `baseline-catalog.json` and the frozen guide. `build-capture-reconciliation.mjs` needs the local private inspection inventory; do not re-run it against an already integrated baseline. `prepare-capture-artwork.py` uses Pillow and NumPy to prepare reviewed sources, `build-capture-spotify-decisions.mjs` combines the reviewed proposals and independent proof cache, and `apply-capture-intake.mjs` reconstructs canonical shards from the immutable baseline. Run both ordinary catalog/mask merge scripts afterward, followed by `npm run check`.

`apply-capture-masks.mjs` is the one-time geometry import; the exact original and new outlines are archived separately. Do not repeat it over current masks. The source mask shards are the editable inputs for later tracing.

Research contains rejected candidates and source responses for audit, excluded from Vercel uploads. The public site includes only selected display assets. Peer reviews are stored alongside the receipt; browser QA records the application checks.
