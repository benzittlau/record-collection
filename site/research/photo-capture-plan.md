# Sequential photo capture plan

Saved plan 7ac3efbb41aa, prepared 2026-10-13. Up to 47 sleeves and 94 photos; 16 entries may be folded edges.

Open [the phone guide](../dist/capture.html) or [the printable visual map](../../output/pdf/record-collection-capture-map.pdf). The guide highlights each requested sleeve in the original photograph, shows its neighbors, and steps through front/back pairs.

## Camera sequence

1. Follow boxes 1 to 5. Within each box, move left to right, section by section.
2. Pull out the highlighted physical sleeve. Photograph its front, then its back, with all four corners and small text in focus.
3. Put it back in its original position before moving on. For box sets, photograph the closed outer box. No disc or gatefold-interior photos are required in this pass.
4. Skip a possible folded edge if it is part of a sleeve already photographed. Use actual jacket titles where outlines overlap. Do not photograph the same jacket twice.
5. Send the original camera files together, in one batch or consecutive batches. Keep the camera filenames and original file order. No captions, renaming, folders or image organization are required. Retakes are fine; keep them beside the originals.

Front photos also establish identity and give a reliable reference for matching the following back, even when a digital front already exists. The original worn sleeve may differ from the reference artwork. Guide numbers are planned shot identifiers, not required camera filenames. After upload, match files by sequence and visible cover content, then confirm uncertain physical positions against the pictured neighboring albums.
