# Artwork research candidates

These images were downloaded during identification and artwork research, but are not the current selected cover, back or LP label in the application. They include superseded digital covers, alternate pressings, record labels, gatefold interiors and rejected CD tray scans. They remain available for audit and future research without being shipped to the website.

`index.json` maps original download paths from research logs to the archived paths and records their checksums. The catalogue and its documented selected image paths determine what is shown in the app.
